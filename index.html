The problem

Every call still asks you to say the unsafe thing out loud.

Reading sensitive information to a stranger on the phone is slow, easy to mishear, and leaves it exposed to whoever's listening — human or recording. It's the same broken moment at every bank, clinic, and call center.

"Can you read me your full Social?"
"I'll need your card number, then the CVV…"
"Let's start with your date of birth."
"Spell that last name for me?"
How it works

Four steps. No information spoken aloud.

The mechanic is the same whether you're talking to a person or an AI agent — pairing, identity, request, approval.

01

Pair the call

The business opens a session and reads you a short code — like a meeting ID, but for this one call.

02

Prove identity

You and the business each sign in with your own PIN. Your key never leaves your device — the PIN just authorizes it to sign.

03

Request specific fields

The business asks for exactly what it needs — SSN last 4, a policy number — never blanket access to everything.

04

Approve, deny, or flag

You see exactly what's being asked and decide in one tap. Nothing moves without your explicit approval.

Security

Built so a stolen code isn't enough.

Field-level requests only

Businesses ask for named fields, never "everything." You see the exact list before approving anything.

Mutual identity verification

Both sides prove who they are with a registered key before any request is even possible.

Cooldowns prevent spam

A business can't flood a caller with requests — limits and lockouts are built into every session.

Mismatch detection

If a signature doesn't match the registered key, the session is flagged and closed — not just retried.

Verified businesses only

Every business passes a credential check — EIN, license, authorized rep — before it can request anything.

Full activity history

Every request, approval, denial, and flag is logged and searchable — for both sides.
